Regions — the parts of the bird you will paint & measure

exclude dark: for a region like the eye, tick this so pixels darker than the L* cutoff (the black pupil, and any deep shadow) are dropped and only the iris colour is measured. Paint the whole eye, then nudge the cutoff until the live panel shows the pupil pixels excluded. Leave it off for chevron/texture regions, where the dark markings are the signal.

Measurements — computed for every region

Click a measurement to include it. Every colour is grey-world white-balanced (neutralising the light) before measuring, and CIELAB tones are read on that balanced image; the R/G/B : photo ratios are read on the original image so the ratio itself cancels lighting.
